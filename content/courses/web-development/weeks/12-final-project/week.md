Think about a chef at the end of a cooking course. She cooks a full meal for guests, using everything she learned. This week, you are that chef.

You have come a long way, from your first HTML tag to apps that fetch live data from the internet. Now you build something that is **yours**. You go from the first idea to a live website you can show anyone.

This week works a little differently. There are fewer lessons and more time for building. The lessons show how real developers work on a project:

- choosing an idea that fits your time
- planning with user stories and sketches
- splitting the work into small tasks
- asking for feedback
- writing a README that makes your project look professional

At the end, you present your project in a short demo. Many people feel nervous about this. That is normal. We give you a simple structure to follow. The audience is friendly, because everyone is doing the same thing.

Last, we look at what comes next: how to keep learning, how to build a portfolio, and how to move towards your first role in tech.

## By the end of this week you will be able to

- Choose a project idea that is the right size, and define a minimum viable product (MVP)
- Plan your project with user stories, a wireframe and a list of GitHub issues
- Work in small steps, commit often, and ask for and respond to code review
- Write a clear README with screenshots and a live link
- Present your project in a short, structured demo
- Make a plan for your next steps in learning and in your career
