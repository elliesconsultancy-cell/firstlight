Welcome to Firstlight! You do not need any experience to start this course. You only need curiosity, a computer, and a little time every week. Everybody here started at zero once, including your instructor.

This first week is not about writing lots of code. It is about getting ready. Think of it like a new job: before you do the real work, you find your desk, learn where things are, and meet the team.

You will learn how a normal week on the course works, install the tools that real web developers use every day, and practise the most important skill in programming: **knowing what to do when you are stuck**. We will also talk honestly about AI tools, and how to use them so that they help you learn instead of doing the learning for you.

At the end of the week you will introduce yourself, so we can get to know you and help you in the best way.

## By the end of this week you will be able to

- Explain the weekly rhythm of the course: read, practise, submit, get feedback
- Open Chrome DevTools and VS Code, and create a folder for your course work
- Use a simple routine to get unstuck: rubber duck, read the error, search, ask
- Write a good question that includes context, what you tried, and what you expected
- Describe when AI tools help your learning and when they hurt it
