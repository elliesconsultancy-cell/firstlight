## What you'll build

This week you won't build a website yet. Instead, you'll tell us a little about yourself, and prove your tools are set up and working. This helps your instructor understand your goals and support you in the right way.

## Requirements

- [ ] Write a short introduction (about 100 to 250 words) in your own words
- [ ] Say why you want to learn coding
- [ ] Share one or two goals for this course (for example "build a website for my small business" or "get a junior developer job")
- [ ] Say roughly how many hours per week you can study, and when
- [ ] Mention anything you are worried about, or anything that would help you learn (optional, but helpful)
- [ ] Take a screenshot of VS Code with your `firstlight` folder open and a file (like `hello.html`) open in the editor

## Steps/hints

1. Make sure you finished the **Setting up your tools** lesson. You should have a `firstlight` folder with a `week-00` folder inside, and a `hello.html` file.
2. Open the `firstlight` folder in VS Code and click on `hello.html` so it is open in the editor. The Explorer on the left should show your folders.
3. Take a screenshot:
   - **Windows:** press `Windows + Shift + S`, then select the area.
   - **Mac:** press `Cmd + Shift + 4`, then drag to select the area.
   - **Linux:** usually the `Print Screen` key, or a "Screenshot" app.
4. Write your introduction. Here are some questions to help you:
   - What is your name, and where are you based?
   - What do you do now (job, studies, caring for family, something else)?
   - Why coding? What made you join?
   - What would make this course a success for you?
   - How many hours a week can you give, and on which days?
5. Write in your own words. Your English does not need to be perfect. We want to hear **you**, so please do not use AI to write this.

> 💡 **Tip:** If something did not install correctly, do not wait! Submit what you have, explain what went wrong, and include the error or a screenshot. Getting help with setup is a perfectly good outcome for this week.

## How to submit

Use the submit form on this page:

- Put your **introduction** in the written answer box.
- **Upload your screenshot** of VS Code as a file.

Your instructor will read your introduction and reply with feedback or approval.

## Stretch goals

- Install the **Live Server** extension in VS Code (search for it in the Extensions panel). It refreshes your browser automatically when you save. Mention in your submission whether you got it working.
- Change the VS Code colour theme to one you like (**File → Preferences → Theme → Color Theme**, or **Code → Settings → Theme** on Mac).
- Open DevTools on your `hello.html` page, find your `<h1>` in the Elements panel, and double-click the text to change it. Take a second screenshot.
