This is it: the final week. You have come a long way, from your first HTML tag to apps that fetch live data from the internet. Now it is time to build something that is **yours**, from the first idea to a live website you can show anyone.

This week works a little differently. There are fewer lessons, and more time for building. The lessons teach you how real developers work on a project: choosing an idea that fits the time you have, planning it with user stories and sketches, splitting the work into small tasks, asking for feedback, and writing a README that makes your project look professional.

At the end, you will present your project in a short demo. Many people feel nervous about this, and that is completely normal. We will give you a simple structure to follow, and the audience will be friendly, because they are all doing the same thing.

Finally, we will look at what comes next: how to keep learning, how to build a portfolio, and how to move towards your first role in tech.

## By the end of this week you will be able to

- Choose a project idea that is the right size, and define a minimum viable product (MVP)
- Plan your project with user stories, a wireframe and a list of GitHub issues
- Work in small steps, commit often, and ask for and respond to code review
- Write a clear README with screenshots and a live link
- Present your project in a short, structured demo
- Make a plan for your next steps in learning and in your career
